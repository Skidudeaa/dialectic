Built-In Discipline

The system doesn't just show you the thesis. It enforces it.

Failure Mode Gates prevent you from front-running trades the framework says aren't ready. The services trade (OIH/HAL/SLB) requires rig count confirmation AND curve spread below 20%. The dashboard enforces this structurally — not willpower, architecture.

Gates & Constraints

Three structural barriers: gates (conditions before a node can fire), constraints (blocks even if gate opens), and deadlines (irreversible date-gated nodes). The planting deadline counts down: miss it, and 93M acres of corn don't get planted.

services.gatedBy = [rig-confirm]
services.constrainedBy = [curve < 20%]

Trading Desk · Causal reasoning engine for macro trading

Data sources: Yahoo Finance v7 spark API, Polymarket Gamma API, manual indicators from Reuters, Baker Hughes, IEA, Dallas Fed, Goldman Sachs, EIA, USDA, BLS, CME Group

Methodology: Directed acyclic graph with expert-specified transmission mechanisms, lags, and strengths. Thresholds are heuristic (calibrated against 2008 and 1979 analogs). Edge strengths are subjective conditional probabilities. Cascade phases use checklist-based signpost tracking.

Limitations: Trigger thresholds are heuristic, not statistically derived. Edge strengths are expert estimates, not empirically calibrated. Monthly rebalancing assumed. Polymarket probabilities may not reflect informed views on niche scenarios.

Research framework for structured expert judgment, not financial advice. All instruments carry risk of loss. The math enforces consistency — it does not generate predictions. · As of 2026-03-30
